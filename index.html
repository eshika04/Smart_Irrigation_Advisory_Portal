Welcome to the Smart Irrigation Advisory System

About the System

The AI-Based Smart Irrigation Advisory System is designed for efficient irrigation management in sugarcane farming. It uses soil moisture sensor readings and weather information to provide irrigation recommendations to farmers.

The system helps farmers monitor important agricultural conditions and make informed decisions about irrigation. By considering soil moisture, temperature, humidity and rainfall information, water can be managed more efficiently.
